# Non-Claims — Fractalish AI v0.1

This prototype does **not** prove or provide:

- consciousness, sentience, or personhood
- artificial general intelligence (AGI)
- medical, legal, or financial diagnosis or advice
- universal morphology decoding
- unique cause identification from form alone
- real-world safety certification
- disaster, failure, or health-event prediction
- replacement for domain experts

## What v0.1 does demonstrate

- local-first activation state with explicit records
- morphology descriptor extraction on synthetic samples
- ternary uncertainty gating (MCVA / HOLD / AMCVA)
- Natural Math baseline vs memory-enabled comparison
- traceable decision records through PERCEPT → GUARD
- SessionGlyph continuity export
- SERA-style cost and waste accounting
