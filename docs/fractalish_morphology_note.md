# Fractalish Morphology Note

Fractalish reads the shape left by process. MCVA is morphology readout — evidence, not proof.

## Classifications

| Morphology | Expected gate | Rationale |
|------------|---------------|-----------|
| branching_trace | MCVA | Structured branching suitable for tentative comparison |
| crack_like_trace | HOLD | Structured but ambiguous; preserve uncertainty |
| irregular_boundary | HOLD | Boundary morphology ambiguous for baseline comparison |
| noise_non_diagnostic | AMCVA | Non-diagnostic; guard against over-reading |

## Doctrine

- MCVA reads cautiously
- HOLD is sacred — irregular or ambiguous form routes to HOLD
- AMCVA guards against noise overclaim
- Morphology is evidence, not proof of cause or intelligence

Claims like "this proves intelligence" route to HOLD or REVERSE via RIGOR overclaim analyzer.
