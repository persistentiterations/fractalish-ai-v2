Why HOLD matters

HOLD before false closure. Unsupported claims, contradictions, and overclaims route to HOLD — uncertainty is preserved, not invented away.

Pressure is not truth. ATAL records urgency, threat, and frustration without converting them into justified belief.

Similarity is not identity. RIGOR warns when comparison is mistaken for sameness.

Three layers

  • Natural Math — bounded process (growth, steps, revisits)
  • Fractalish / MCVA — morphology readout (MCVA / HOLD / AMCVA)
  • Cognitive Basin — state discipline and guard across process
